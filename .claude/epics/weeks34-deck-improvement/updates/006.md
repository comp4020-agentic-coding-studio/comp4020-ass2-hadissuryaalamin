and by opening the resized files:

  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chateaubriand_cut.jpg), CC BY-SA

  2019:11:02 03:43:43, real lens/exposure data — no AI-generation tell.
  Original 6000×4000, resized to 960×640. File shows the tenderloin/loin cut
  into portions with fine, even grain, no knife or hand in frame.

Rejected candidates, and why:
- File:Cutting Beef Chuck.jpg (chuck, Nikon D3400, legitimate EXIF) — visually
  shows a hand actively cutting the meat with a knife, which conflicts with
  the task's "shape and consequence only, never execution" rule. Used
  `beef-shank.jpg` instead, which shows the whole worked cut with no hand or
  knife in frame.
- File:Striploin.jpg (993×492, 2006 upload, no camera EXIF) — visually the
  cut is wrapped in film and shows fat cap/skin rather than exposed grain;
  too low-resolution and doesn't carry the fine-grain argument as clearly as

- File:Beef tenderloin.jpg — only 148×148 px, far below the ~960 px
  convention; rejected on resolution alone.
- File:Striploin bull1.jpg — 635×459 px, below the convention; description
  only in Russian; no camera EXIF (Photoshop-only metadata); rejected on
  resolution.
