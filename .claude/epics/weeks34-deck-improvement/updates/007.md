## What was sourced, and from where

Both from Wikimedia Commons, verified by direct `action=query&prop=imageinfo`
lookups (URL, extmetadata license, size, EXIF) and by opening each resized
file before use:

- `fridge-thermometer.jpg` ← File:Food Safety - Cut Waste in Refrigerators
  (20120106-OC-AMW-0073).jpg
  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Food_Safety_-_Cut_Waste_in_Refrigerators_(20120106-OC-AMW-0073).jpg),
  **Public domain** (US government work, USDAgov). EXIF: Phase One digital
  back, model "H 25" — a real medium-format camera consistent with
  professional USDA stock photography — capture timestamp
  2006-02-28 16:40:50, no AI-generation tell. Original 5436×4080, resized
  to 960×721.
- `beef-rib-roast.jpg` ← File:Standing-rib-roast.jpg
  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Standing-rib-roast.jpg),
  CC BY-SA 2.5, author Michael C. Berch ("MCB"). EXIF: Canon PowerShot
  SD500, capture timestamp 2006-11-25 16:54:49 (predates any
  AI-generation concern entirely), no GPS/placeholder-timestamp tell.
  Native size 935×679 — already under the ~960 px convention, so no
  resize was applied (upscaling a legitimately-smaller source was judged
  worse than leaving it at its real resolution).

Rejected candidates, and why:

- File:Roast topside of beef.jpg — real EXIF (Canon EOS 6D, 2017), CC
  BY-SA 4.0, but visually a **cooked**, sliced roast (pink medium-rare
  interior). Every storage-window claim on this slide concerns raw meat;
  pairing raw ground beef with a cooked joint would misrepresent the
  material. Rejected on inspection after downloading.
- File:Beef Meat.jpg and File:MEAT IN MARKET.jpg — both have plausible
  phone-camera EXIF (iPhone 6 / iPhone 11 Pro Max) so pass the
  AI-generation check, but visually show informal, unrefrigerated market
  butchery (one shows a carcass being cut on the ground) — wrong register
  for the course's deadpan academic voice and could visually read as an
  example of *bad* practice rather than a neutral illustration. Rejected
  on inspection.
- File:Fresh Beef Shanks.jpg — only 696×1018 and the direct file download
  failed (near-empty response); not pursued further once beef-rib-roast.jpg
  was confirmed suitable.
- Rubbermaid R80DC Refrigerator Freezer Thermometer (Flickr via Tony
  Webster, CC BY 2.0) — a viable alternative to the USDA photo for the
  4 °C check, real EXIF (Canon EOS 5D Mark III, 2017), but the USDA public
  domain photo was preferred: no attribution-license friction, and it
  visually includes raw poultry in the same fridge, reinforcing "keep at
  or below 4 °C" without adding a second unrelated claim.

No candidate was found or used that duplicates week 10's `meat-thermometer.jpg`
subject (a meat-insertion probe thermometer) — both images used here are an
ambient appliance/fridge thermometer, a different instrument and framing.

Credit lines recorded in `src/decks/assets/week-04/SOURCES.md`, appended
