## Sources cited

  (<https://www.fda.gov/media/74435/download>, March 2018). Used for:
  - whole poultry and poultry parts both held at 1–2 days refrigerated,

## What was deliberately left unstated

**No holding-time figure is claimed for fish portion form** (fillet vs
steak vs whole fish). The FDA storage chart — the only authoritative
source checked — distinguishes fish storage exclusively by fat content
(lean vs fatty), never by whether the fish is left whole or cut into
fillets/steaks. Rather than borrow a number from elsewhere or invent
one, the deck says this explicitly on the "What the eight-piece
breakdown costs" slide: "No equivalent figure is documented for fish
portion form... so no holding-time cost is claimed for fish portioning
here." This mirrors the epic's instruction to state the absence of a
number plainly rather than paper over it.

The poultry side is *not* an invented number either — both the 1–2 day
refrigerated window and the 9-month/1-year frozen-quality figures come
directly from the FDA chart already cited elsewhere in this deck.
