Decision: weeks 9 and 10 discuss the week-2/week-4 material as **prose
callbacks only** ("week 2 separated produce into...", "week 4's red meat
material follows the same pattern...") with no frontmatter `related:` edge
to those unconfirmed slugs. The edges that *were* confirmable
(`lectures/week-03` for week 3's poultry/fish figures, `sessions/09-fruit`
↔ `sessions/10-food-safety-and-the-cold-chain` between my own files) are
declared.

**Action for integration / whoever runs the final `pnpm check`:** once
task 001's session files for weeks 2 and 4 exist, consider adding
`related: [sessions/<week-2-slug>]` to `09-fruit.md` and
`related: [sessions/<week-2-slug>, sessions/<week-4-slug>]` to

cross-link intent. This is a nice-to-have follow-up, not a blocker — every
