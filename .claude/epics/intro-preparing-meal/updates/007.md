## Schema note

The `people` collection schema in `src/content.config.ts` does not include a
`related` field (unlike `courseNodeSchema`, which `sessions`/`assessments`/
`lectures` extend) and is not `.loose()`, so Astro's content-collection
parser silently strips `related:` from the typed `data` object. This does
not affect the course graph: `astro-course-university`'s
`readCourseNodes()` (`course-content.ts`) reads `related:` straight out of
each file's raw YAML frontmatter, independent of the Zod schema, and `people`
is listed in `graphCollections` in `src/site-config.ts`. So the `related:`
edges in these two files reach `dist/api/index.json` correctly at build time
even though they're invisible to `getCollection("people")` consumers.
