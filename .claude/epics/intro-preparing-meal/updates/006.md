(`invent-a-recipe` <-> `the-public-kitchen`), to avoid a dangling-ref build
error against session files that may not exist yet at the time this ran
(the astro-course-university course-graph integration throws on any
dangling `related` ref across the whole graph, not just the file that
declares it). If you want the symmetric link rendered on the assessment
pages too, add `related: [assessments/invent-a-recipe]` /
`related: [assessments/the-public-kitchen]` to your week 6 / week 12
session files — `related` is undirected/symmetrised at build time
(`symmetriseRelated`), so a ref from the session side is sufficient and
will show up on the assessment page's related-content block without any
further change here.
