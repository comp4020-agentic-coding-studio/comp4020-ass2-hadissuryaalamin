## Alt text — viewed the actual file, did not invent

Read `src/decks/assets/week-11/thermometer-cooking.jpg` directly (the
`Read` tool renders image files visually). It is a GEFU-brand dial oven
thermometer lying on a grey surface: a round dial face with a Celsius scale
(roughly 50–300°) and, on an outer ring, printed icons for cattle, pig and
chicken bracketing red-marked temperature bands for each; a long metal
probe extends from the right side of the housing; the needle sits at
roughly 90 °C.

**Judgment call:** the epic's own §2 text suggested alt text describing
"probe placement, dial/digital reading" — phrasing that implies the photo
shows the probe inserted into meat. The actual photograph does not show
that; it is the thermometer alone, unused, photographed on a plain
surface. I wrote the alt text from what the image actually shows rather
than from the epic's assumed framing, per the standing house rule (every
prior epic: write alt text from the real image, never invent detail).
Flagging this as a deviation from the epic's literal wording, not from its
intent — the slide's own prose already states the "probe against a minimum
internal temperature" argument; the image's job is to show what the
instrument itself looks like, which it does honestly.
