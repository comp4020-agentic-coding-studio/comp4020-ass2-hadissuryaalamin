# Cut shapes as a storage argument

Scope: `src/decks/week-02.deck.mdx` (lines ~52–58 and the slides that
replace them), `src/decks/assets/week-02/*.jpg` (new files),
`src/decks/assets/week-02/SOURCES.md`.

Read epic.md §2 and the "Image sourcing" section in full before starting.

1. Rewrite `## What this week leaves out` (currently defers all cutting to
   week 7). Replace with slide(s) covering julienne, batonnet, brunoise,
   small dice, chiffonade — each with a sourced photograph and, where a
   real culinary reference gives it, its conventional dimensions.
2. Work the carrot through explicitly across the cut shapes.
3. State the storage consequence per cut: more cut surface per gram means
   faster moisture loss and faster microbial access. This is what keeps
   the slide inside week 2's remit (keeping quality) rather than week 7's
   (technique) — do not describe knife grip or execution.
4. **No invented numbers.** A cut dimension or a storage-life figure is a
   factual claim and needs a source that resolves. If no citable source
   exists for a given number, state the point qualitatively and omit the
   number.
5. Do not touch `src/decks/week-07.deck.mdx`. Its cut-shapes table stays as
   duplication, per epic decision 3 — this is deliberate, not an oversight.
6. Every new image: Commons or another free source, visually checked,
   EXIF-checked for AI-generation tells, ~1280px wide, alt text written,
   one credit line added to `SOURCES.md` in the existing format.
