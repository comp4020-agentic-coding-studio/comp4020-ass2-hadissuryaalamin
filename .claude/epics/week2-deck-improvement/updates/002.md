## Dimensions: sourced vs. omitted

Sourced from Auguste Escoffier School of Culinary Arts (resolves, verified
by fetch):

- Julienne: 3 mm × 3 mm, 65 mm long
- Batonnet: 6 mm × 6 mm, 65 mm long
- Brunoise: 3 mm cubes
- Small dice: 6 mm cubes

Omitted: Chiffonade has no standard ribbon width documented in any
culinary reference checked for this deck. The slide says so plainly rather
than inventing a number.

Storage-consequence claim: FDA treats cut produce as more time-sensitive
than whole produce (refrigerate once cut, cut away damaged areas) — this
citation supports the qualitative claim only. The surface-area-to-volume
mechanism itself (more exposed surface per gram → faster moisture loss and
microbial access) is stated as reasoning, not attributed to a measured
figure, per the task's "no invented numbers" rule. FoodSafety.gov and USDA
FSIS pages that looked promising in search snippets returned HTTP 403 to
both curl and WebFetch and were not cited, since I couldn't verify they
resolve.
