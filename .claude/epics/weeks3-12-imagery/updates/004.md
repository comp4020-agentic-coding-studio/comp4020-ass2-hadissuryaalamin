## SVGs — the priority-one fix

### Week 7 — blade-anatomy diagram ("The blade in profile")

**Before:** `viewBox="0 0 700 240"` inside a `display: flex` wrapper with a
percentage-width `<svg>`. The flex formatting context ignored the intended
scaling and the svg fell back to the browser's 300px intrinsic default,
rendering at **~360×124 canvas px** — labels (spine, edge, bevel, heel, tip,
bolster, tang) were unreadable.

**After:** moved to its own slide, wrapper changed to a plain block `<div>`,
`<svg>` given an explicit pixel `width="1000"` plus `max-width: 100%; height:
auto; max-height: 420px` in its style. Renders at **1000×343 canvas px**
(aspect-locked from the 700:240 viewBox). Screenshot confirms all seven labels
legible with no clipping (`tmp-verify/shots/week07-blade-svg.png`, viewport
1280×720, canvas maxBottom well under 720).

`aria-label` text unchanged: "Labelled diagram of a chef's knife in profile,
showing the spine, edge, bevel, heel, tip, bolster and tang."

### Week 8 — sequencing timeline ("The order, timed")

**Before:** `viewBox="0 0 760 120"` inside a `display: flex` wrapper, `<svg
width="700">`, `max-height: 150px`. Same flex-context bug as week 7 capped the
effective render around **~110px** tall.

**After:** own slide, block `<div>` wrapper, `<svg width="1280">`,
`max-width: none; height: auto; max-height: 260px`. At a 760:120 (6.33:1)
aspect ratio the 1280px canvas width is the binding constraint, so it renders
at **1280×202 canvas px**. Screenshot confirms all four boxes and their arrows
legible (`tmp-verify/shots/week08-timeline-svg.png`).

`aria-label` text unchanged: "Timeline strip showing the four preparation
categories in order: finished ahead, held, made to order, sequenced."

Both fixes use the same pattern established across the epic: a flex wrapper
plus percentage-width svg silently falls back to a 300px intrinsic size inside
Reveal's grid-display sections — replace the wrapper with a plain block `<div>`
and give the `<svg>` an explicit pixel `width` attribute.

- **"Deciding the order of work"** — immediately precedes "The order, timed",
  which already carries the enlarged sequencing SVG. A second image here would
  be decorative duplication of the diagram's own claim, not new evidence.
  Skipped.
- **"Closing the loop on week 1"** — an abstract argument slide connecting
  week 8 back to week 1's time-budget claim; no discrete photographable

  Skipped.
