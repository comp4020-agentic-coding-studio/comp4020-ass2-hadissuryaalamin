Two of these six needed a variant of the pattern, not the plain prototype,
because the epic's own measured 13-line/571px text-cell budget
(`updates/001.md` §3) was tight or exceeded if the whole slide's prose went
into the `.columns` text cell:

- **"Poultry"** carries a two-row markdown table before the paragraph. Tables
  aren't covered by the `.columns :is(p, li)` helper (task 001 scoped it to
  `p`/`li` only), and pushing a table into a 544px cell was an unmeasured
  risk. Kept the table full-width, unchanged, directly under the heading;
  wrapped only the trailing paragraph + image in `.columns` below it. Measured
  text bottom 518.0px canvas-relative — comfortable, 202px of headroom.
- **"What the eight-piece breakdown costs"** has two paragraphs (~70 words +
  ~46 words). Estimated combined wrapped-line count at column width exceeded
  the ~13-line budget. Kept the first (longer) paragraph full-width,
  unchanged, directly under the heading; wrapped only the second paragraph +
  image in `.columns`. Measured text bottom 539.1px — fits with 181px spare.

Neither of these split slides changed a word of prose — only where the
`.columns` boundary starts. No slide's paragraph was reordered, shortened, or
turned into a different structure (e.g. no prose was converted into bullets).
