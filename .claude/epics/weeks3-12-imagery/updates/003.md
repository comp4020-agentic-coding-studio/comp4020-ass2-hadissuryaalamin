**Status: completed.** This is a retry of a lane that died mid-flight on a

## The seven orphaned files

`src/decks/assets/week-05/{basil,bay-leaf,lime-leaf,oregano}.jpg` and
`src/decks/assets/week-06/{chef-knife,colander,food-container}.jpg` existed on
disk with no `SOURCES.md` entry and no record of where they came from.

- **`week-05/basil.jpg` — deleted, confirmed bad.** EXIF `Copyright` field read
  `"Copyright Wdnet Studio & AS Photography. All Rights Reserved."` — an
  explicit rights claim, not licensing boilerplate. Checked with Python/PIL
  (`Image.open(f).getexif()`). A separate byte-scan hit on
  `bay-leaf.jpg` ("Copyright 1999 Adobe Systems Incorporated") was
  investigated and is the standard Adobe RGB (1998) ICC profile string that
  ships with countless freely-licensed photos — not a photograph rights
  claim, so it was not treated as disqualifying on its own.
- **The other six — deleted, unverifiable.** None could be matched to a
  specific Commons file by dimensions, filename or metadata. Two
  (`chef-knife.jpg` at 960×1477, `colander.jpg` at 960×720) happened to match
  the scaled dimensions of a plausible Commons candidate, but `basil.jpg`
  showed an identical dimension coincidence while being proven copyright
  violated — 3:2/4:3 ratios at a 960px thumbnail width are too common for
  dimension match alone to count as evidence. Rather than salvage two of the
  six on a coincidence, all six were deleted and replaced uniformly.

All seven were replaced with fresh downloads, each found via the Wikimedia

from the API response, not guessed), visually confirmed by looking at the

**Why "Bay leaf, lime leaf, oregano" became a new slide rather than adding
the `.compare` row to the existing "Early or late, and why" bullets slide:**
tried the row-under-bullets version first and measured it. The bullets slide
alone (heading + 4 bullets + closing paragraph, plain full-width, unmodified
prose) already occupies `top: 64` to `bottom: 913` of vertical space once the
`.compare` row is inserted between the bullets and the closing paragraph —
193px past the 720px canvas, confirmed by the same canvas-relative
measurement used throughout this epic (real overflow, not caught by
`pnpm check`'s text-overflow detector, exactly per `updates/001.md`'s
warning that screenshots are the only thing that catches this). The bullets'
own height (358px, full 1.75rem body text, unmodified) plus the closing
sentence leaves no room for a further ~280px image row without either
shrinking the `.compare` images below the 200px floor or reordering/rewording
prose — both against the hard rules. Moving the three images to a new slide
directly after the bullets slide adds no prose, changes no figure, table or
citation on the existing slide, and fits cleanly (heading + `.compare` row
only, well inside 720px). `epic.md`'s "no slide-count assumption" was
confirmed by checking `spec/course-promises.test.ts`, which only checks that
each lecture's `meta.slides` deck path resolves to a built page — it does
not assert a slide count.
