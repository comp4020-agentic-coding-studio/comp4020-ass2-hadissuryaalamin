### The six fixes

One decision, applied per-image with inline styles as the epic requires. **No
prose, figure, table, citation, alt text or asset file was touched, and no
slide was split or added.**

The sizing rule used: on a clipped slide the image's *visible* height is
already only `720 − image top`, so capping `max-height` at that visible height
removes the clipping **without losing anything a viewer could previously
see**. Every fix therefore costs zero visible image area and every result
stays at or above the 200px floor.

| Deck | Image | `max-height` | Rendered after | Slide bottom after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| week-03 | `chicken-leg-quarters.jpg` | 480 → **330px** | 440×330 | 712.6 |
| week-03 | `fridge-thermometer.jpg` | 480 → **320px** | 426×320 | 713.0 |
| week-04 | `beef-shank.jpg` | none → **240px** | 320×240 | 706.8 |
| week-04 | `beef-rib-roast.jpg` | none → **210px** | 289×210 | 706.8 |
| week-04 | `ground-beef.jpg` | none → **210px** | 276×210 | 706.8 |
| week-10 | `meat-thermometer.jpg` | 480 → **200px** | 544×200 | 708.8 |
| week-11 | `thermometer-cooking.jpg` | 220 → **205px** | 227×205 | 711.8 |

The two `.compare` images in week 4 previously carried no inline style at all
and were sized by the theme's `.compare figure img { max-height: 19rem }`
(304px) — which is exactly why lane A's report shows them as "bigger without
any per-image style". They were bigger than the canvas allowed.

**Week 10's is a partial fix and should be read as such.** Lane D's
priority-one change took `meat-thermometer.jpg` from 80px to a nominal 480px,
but only 211px of it was ever on screen. Capping at 200px makes the slide
honest and still 2.5× the original, yet it does not deliver the intended
480px. The slide cannot carry a 480px image *and* its intro line, 2-row
table and two paragraphs inside 720px. Growing it needs a slide split, which
needs a new heading — new prose, which this task is forbidden to write.
**Flagged for the user as follow-up work, not silently accepted.**
