## Screenshot verification (beyond what `pnpm check` can see)

astromotion's own `deck-check.mjs` explicitly measures overflow only for
text-bearing elements (`h1, h2, h3, h4, h5, h6, p, ul, ol, pre, table,
blockquote, dl` — see its own code comment: "Backgrounds, split-image
panels and decorative art bleed off the canvas by design, so only
text-bearing elements are measured for overflow"). Images and SVGs are
deliberately out of its scope. Per this project's own carried-forward
lesson ("screenshots catch what every automated check misses"), wrote a
Playwright script matching astromotion's own methodology (`.reveal
.slides > section.present` selector, canvas-relative scale conversion via
`scale = box.height / 720`, one-based hash navigation, `document.fonts
.ready` wait, 4px tolerance) but extended to check `img`/`svg` overflow
and failed image loads, across every slide of both decks at 1280x720 and
375x812, listening for `pageerror`/`console.error`.

Two real clipping defects found and fixed, both invisible to `pnpm
check`:

- **`garlic.jpg`** (week-05, "What intact tissue keeps apart", slide 4 of
  18) ran 143px past the 720px canvas bottom at the original `max-height:
  220px`. Confirmed visually via screenshot before fixing. First fix
  attempt (`90px`) still overflowed by 7px; reduced further to `75px` —
  re-verified clean.
- **`plastic-cutting-board.jpg`** (week-06, "Plastic", slide 6 of 17) ran
  35px past the canvas bottom at `220px`. Confirmed visually via
  screenshot. Fixed to `170px` on the first attempt — re-verified clean.
- `olive-oil.jpg` (week-05) had no clipping issue at any point.
