Choosing which fruit to build a dish around is, among other things, a calorie-density decision — this week's lecture covers where each fruit is stored and why; this exercise covers what each one actually contributes once it is in the dish.

## Exercise

Select two fruits below at the same quantity — a banana and a serving of strawberries, say — and compare calorie content. Then check whether the climacteric fruits here (apple, banana, mango) differ systematically on any of the three figures from the two non-climacteric fruits (orange, strawberry), or whether ripening behaviour and nutritional composition are simply unrelated questions.

<MacroCalculator data={week09} headingLevel="h3" />

None of the five fruits is a meaningful source of fat or protein; the spread in calories across them tracks sugar content, not the climacteric split this week's lecture uses to decide where each one is stored.
