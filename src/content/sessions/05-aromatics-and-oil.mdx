Deciding how long a chopped or crushed allium can wait before it must go into the pan or the refrigerator is a preparation decision distinct from what the cutting itself does to its flavour, which is the lecture's subject.

## Exercise

Enter a preparation time for a chopped or crushed garlic, onion or shallot held at room temperature and read off its safe-until time. Repeat for the same item refrigerated, then for a prepared leaf herb, to see how differently the three clocks run for material handled in the same week.

<StorageCalculator data={week05Storage} />

Read the note attached to each refrigerated result: USDA publishes no allium- or herb-specific refrigerated figure, so the calculator applies its general window for cut, perishable food at the shorter, more conservative end. Teaching the concept without a fabricated number is the same rule this course applies everywhere a figure cannot be sourced.
