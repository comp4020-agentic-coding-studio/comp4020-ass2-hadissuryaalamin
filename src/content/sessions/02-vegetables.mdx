A vegetable's contribution to a dish is a preparation decision measured in grams, not guessed from how substantial it looks on a plate — this week's lecture covers where a vegetable is stored and why; this exercise covers what it is actually made of.

## Exercise

Pick a vegetable below and a quantity plausible for a dish — a handful of spinach, a diced potato, a sliced carrot — and read off its fat, protein and calorie content. Repeat with a second vegetable at the same quantity and compare the two: vegetables that look similarly substantial on a plate can differ by a factor of three or more in calorie density, for reasons unrelated to anything this week's lecture covers about storage.
