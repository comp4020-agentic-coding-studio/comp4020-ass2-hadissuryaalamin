Every material this course has stored since week 2 answers to the same clock; this exercise is where that clock is actually run, rather than only stated as a range of days.

## Exercise

Choose a raw material below — poultry, ground meat, a whole cut, or an already-prepared leftover — enter the time it was refrigerated, and read off the time by which it must be reheated to a safe temperature, refrozen or discarded. Repeat for a material held at room temperature to see how sharply the danger-zone rule shortens the same clock.

<StorageCalculator data={week10Storage} />

Compare the safe-until time for raw poultry against a whole cut of red meat prepared at the same moment: the gap between the two is the anatomical argument from weeks 3 and 4, expressed here as a difference in hours rather than in muscle structure.
