Choosing a fat percentage for ground beef is a preparation decision with a measurable nutritional consequence, made at the point of purchase rather than at the pan — the same reckoning this week's lecture applies to whole cuts, applied here to the blends a recipe actually names by number.

## Exercise

Select the two ground-beef entries below in turn — 85% lean and 93% lean — at the same quantity, and compare their fat and calorie figures. Then check lamb leg and goat the same way: goat's low fat content is easy to underestimate against the other three from appearance alone.

<MacroCalculator data={week04} headingLevel="h3" />

Rank all four by fat content. Goat's smaller muscle groups and lower external fat, discussed in the lecture, should place it last; if it does not, check the quantity entered before assuming the figure is wrong.
